Direction 6 · Sahara Night ليل الصحراء

A game played on a woven mat in the desert after sunset. Seats are lantern medallions whose golden ring burns down as the turn clock; the score is a caravan walking a 21-step track to the oasis; capture options are numbered stars; a CHKOBBA is a shooting star that writes شكبّة in gold. Default deck: Sahara Night. Try the deck menu above, or tap the Deck row on the home screen to open the in-app picker.

What makes it different

  • Lantern seats. The ring around the active player's medallion burns from full to empty (the turn clock); a small flame marks whose turn it is, and the seconds sit in a badge so the timer never depends on colour alone.
  • Caravan score. Two dotted tracks of 21 steps end at the oasis. A glance shows who is closer to winning, which a pair of numbers does not.
  • Star chips. Capture options are four-point stars 1/2/3 in gold, green and violet, distinct from teal (us) and ember (them).
  • Shooting-star CHKOBBA. A meteor crosses, the swept cards drift up into the sky, then شكبّة is hand-written in gold strokes.
  • Night ledger. The round summary lights one star per point won.

React Native notes

  • Star field and dunes: one pre-rendered background image per screen size; the mat is a radial gradient (Skia) or an image.
  • Burning ring: react-native-svg arc animated by Reanimated, same as a ring timer.
  • Handwritten شكبّة: an SVG text path with strokeDashoffset; fall back to a fade under reduce-motion.
  • Fonts: Marcellus (display), Inter (body), Reem Kufi (Arabic).

Risks

  • Dark and calm: less energetic than Neon Souk, might feel slow for competitive players.
  • The round mat leaves less room for table cards than a rectangle; more than 9 cards needs a second ring.
  • The palm emoji at the oasis is a placeholder; use a drawn icon in production.
