Deck designs · thirteen decks, one picker

The deck is its own cosmetic slot, separate from the table theme. A player can sit at the Café table with the Neon deck, or play Mergoum with Jumbo cards. Every deck is drawn from SVG and text only, scaled from a single width, so the same face works at table size (46 px), hand size (78 px) and in the shop. Every deck gives the 7 of diamonds (Sabaa el Haya, worth a point on its own) a special mark, so players can spot it at a glance. The free decks work as accessibility, learning and seasonal options; the premium decks are shop items. The five decks marked New (Sejnane, Fouta, Riso Zine, Sahara Night, Harissa Pop) come from chkobba-decks-2.js. Use the bar above to put every deck on a different table colour. Court cards always count Q = 8, J = 9, K = 10.

The picker

Settings → Cards. Tap a deck in the strip to preview it: the fan and the live table on the right update. Free and owned decks equip right away. Locked decks show their price in credits (cosmetic only: never wagered, never converted).

Same sizes as the app's Café skin: table cards are 46×66, your hand is 78×112, and opponents' backs are 28×40. A capturable card has a highlight ring, to check the deck still reads under it.

All decks

Each deck shows its back and seven sample cards (ace, the 7♦, two number cards and the three court cards) at hand size, then the same cards at table size, which is the real legibility test.

Recommendation

Ship Café Classic, Jumbo Four-Colour and Khatt free from day one. Jumbo is the best accessibility win: four suit colours make the Dineri count obvious, and court cards print their capture value. Khatt teaches beginners that Q/J/K are 8/9/10. Then release premium decks in pairs with the matching table theme, such as Mergoum table with Mergoum deck, or Neon Souk with Neon. Each pair is a shop bundle, and each deck still works on its own.

  • Code shape: move CardFace and CardBack out of Skin.components into a new src/decks/ registry (Deck = { id, name, premium, CardFace, CardBack }). Each skin gets a defaultDeck, and the player's choice overrides it. Card sizes stay skin tokens, since they are layout. Decks just render into any width, the way CafeCardFace already takes a size.
  • React Native cost: everything here maps to react-native-svg, including the patterns used by Mosaic. The only hard parts are the Neon glow (CSS drop-shadow; in RN, use an SVG blur filter or pre-rendered glow sprites) and the Pixel 7♦ blink (Animated opacity, off under reduced motion).
