Direction 2 · Sidi Bou Said · سيدي بوسعيد

Blue-and-white village palette, an arched table borrowed from the famous blue doors and windows, zellige tile geometry, round team-ringed avatars (blue = us, orange = them), chunky rounded buttons, and emblem face cards (crown, jasmine, chechia). In frame b, tap a hand card, then tap a capture option to preview it; tap again to confirm.

b · Your turn, card selected

UX improvements proposed

  • Colour-coded capture options. Option 1 is blue, option 2 green; the same colour and number appear as badges on the table cards it takes. Tap once to preview (only those cards stay lit), tap again to confirm.
  • Turn callout. The active avatar grows, emits a pulsing team-coloured halo and wears a yellow speech-bubble ("Your turn!" / "Karim is thinking..."), and idle seats soften. Impossible to miss, no text-only status line.
  • Who-just-played tag. The newest table card carries a small avatar bubble of whoever played it plus a "NEW" tag; the log is a chat-style pill under the cards.
  • Team score cards with progress to 21, plus pile (cards captured) and sweep chips per team. Chips bump when they change.
  • Chkobba celebration. Starburst + ribbon banner, falling jasmine petals, the swept cards fly to the sweeper, and a "+1" bubble lands on their avatar. An opponent sweep would use the orange team colour and skip petals.

React Native notes

  • Arched board = borderTopLeftRadius/TopRightRadius. Zellige tiling = react-native-svg Pattern; card back uses the same trick.
  • Avatar ring = nested Views; halo = looping Animated scale/opacity. Starburst rotation = Animated rotate on an SVG.
  • "Chunky" buttons use a solid bottom-offset shadow (a second View under the button), which avoids relying on shadow rendering differences between iOS and Android.
  • Fonts: Fredoka (UI), Cairo (Arabic).

Risks

  • Light backgrounds hurt in dim rooms and burn battery on OLED; a dark-mode variant would need its own pass.
  • The arched board loses vertical room: with 10 table cards (3 rows) the table area gets tight on small phones.
  • Number cards use a single big pip instead of classic pip layouts, so traditionalists may find it less "card-like".
  • Cultural motifs (door, jasmine, chechia, zellige) must be rendered respectfully and consistently; commissioning real illustration is advisable for store assets.
