Neon Souk features (prototype)

  • Sign-plate seats. Idle seats are unlit outlines. The active seat flickers on, fills its team colour (cyan us, pink them) and a neon tube drains around the plate with the seconds in a badge. The text label and seconds carry the signal, glow is decoration.
  • Combo meter. Ten cells charge cyan with each consecutive team capture; chkobbas add gold cells. An opponent sweep resets it to "STREAK LOST" (frame c). Cosmetic only, never affects scoring.
  • Numbered capture chips. Chips 1/2/3 use gold, green and violet (distinct from the team colours); the same number and ring appear on the table cards, and each row previews the cards it takes at 30px. Tap to preview, tap again to confirm.
  • Emotes. Thumbs up, "Yalla!", laugh and facepalm sit beside your plate and pop a bubble above your hand.
  • CHKOBBA surge. One soft screen flash, then a hanging sign spells C-H-K-O-B-B-A letter by letter with a buzz-flicker and lights the Arabic. Suppressed under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Deck switcher. Every card and back is rendered through one cardHTML() / backHTML() pair, so all screens swap together.

React Native notes

  • Glow = layered textShadow (works on iOS and Android Text) and shadowColor/shadowRadius on iOS plus elevation fallback on Android, where coloured shadows are weaker; draw a second blurred Skia or SVG stroke if needed.
  • Tube timer = react-native-svg Rect with strokeDasharray/strokeDashoffset animated by Reanimated, same approach as a ring timer.
  • Perspective grid = pre-rendered SVG or a Skia path, not a live 3D transform, to protect low-end Android.
  • Fonts: Chakra Petch (display), Inter (body), Cairo (Arabic).

Risks

  • Heavy glow costs battery on LCD phones and can read as busy; needs a "reduced glow" setting alongside reduced motion.
  • Flicker and flash must stay well under 3 flashes per second, and be opt-out; tested only against the reduced-motion media query here.
  • Very different tone from the warmer themes: it signals competitive, which may put off casual or older players.
