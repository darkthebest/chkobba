Direction 1 · Café Tunisien · مقهى

A game at a Tunis café: wooden table rim, green baize, cream cards with classic face cards (Ray with a crown, Moujira with a jasmine headband, Kawel in a chechia), brass accents, and a paper racham for the score. UX improvements proposed

  • Numbered capture badges. Each capture option gets a number (1, 2) and the same number is stamped on the table cards it would take. A card in two options (5♣) wears both. Removes the "which cards does this button mean?" guesswork.
  • Last-played card marker. The newest table card has a dashed brass outline and a small "Karim" flag, so you can see what changed since your last turn.
  • Lit nameplate + lantern glow for turns. The active seat's medallion glows and its brass plate lights up; your own turn adds a gold "Your turn" pill. Colour, motion and text all say it.
  • Captured-pile counters (Karta count) under each racham column, so the Karta race is visible during play.
  • Chkobba stamp. A rubber-stamp banner with confetti and the captured cards flying to the sweeper's pile. For an opponent sweep, the same stamp would be muted with no confetti.

React Native notes

  • Wood: expo-linear-gradient plus a handful of 1px absolutely-positioned plank lines. Baize: gradient + borderWidth + inset shadow faked with a second inner View.
  • Suits, pips, face busts, card-back lattice and tally strokes are all react-native-svg (Symbol/Use, Pattern).
  • Dashed borders, rotation, shadows and glow (iOS shadow / Android elevation) are supported. The glow pulse is a looping Animated.Value.
  • Fonts via expo-font: Playfair Display, Lora, Caveat, Amiri (Arabic).

Risks

  • Most visually rich direction: highest asset and polish cost, and the most text styles to localise.
  • Fine pips on table-size cards (46px) are small; the large indices carry readability.
  • Skeuomorphic style can feel dated to younger players and is less ownable in app stores.
